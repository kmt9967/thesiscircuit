# Official Rules Snapshot

Reviewed initially on 2026-08-28 PKT. The current execution-critical snapshot now lives in `docs/HACKATHON-RULES.md` and was rechecked on 2026-08-30 against the new official Alpaca FAQ.

## Event-specific requirements

- Event: Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon, online, August 28–September 4, 2026.
- Submission deadline: September 4, 2026 at 8:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time.
- Build an autonomous AI trading agent using Alpaca's Trading API.
- Use either Alpaca's MCP server or Alpaca CLI.
- Every strategy must incorporate options trading.
- Develop and test in Alpaca's paper-trading environment; no real capital is involved.
- Development may use any paper account, but final judging requires a brand-new dedicated Alpaca paper account. Existing/reused accounts are ineligible.
- The competition paper account must start at exactly $100,000.
- Include a one-page write-up covering AI logic, risk gates, and Alpaca infrastructure.
- Teams may contain 1–6 people.

## Submission requirements

- Project title, short description, long description, technology/category tags.
- 16:9 PNG/JPG cover image.
- Video presentation (general guideline: link, no more than 5 minutes and 300 MB) and PDF slide presentation.
- Public GitHub repository.
- Hosted interactive application URL; event guidance names Streamlit, Replit, or Vercel.
- Alpaca paper-trading account ID used for the hackathon.
- Up to five optional X or LinkedIn build-in-public links tagging Lablab and Alpaca.
- Original, open-source, MIT-compliant work. Plagiarism, vote gaming, tampering, unauthorized automation, or fraud can disqualify a submission.

## Judging criteria

- P&L performance in the submitted Alpaca paper environment.
- Technology implementation, including required Alpaca technology.
- Creativity and originality.
- Presentation and execution.
- Social-engagement content and engagement for that component.

## Prize eligibility and payment

- Main pool: $6,000 ($2,500 / $1,500 / $1,000 main prizes; two $500 social-engagement team prizes plus one-month Algo Trader Plus per member).
- Participants must be 18+. Alpaca employees, contractors, immediate family/household members, and sanctioned-country participants are excluded; void where prohibited.
- Prizes are paid to individuals. Tax, identity, bank, sanctions-screening, and withholding requirements apply; payment may take up to 90 days after the event/documents clear.
- Sponsor/event terms may be amended or the event/prizes cancelled. Judging is final.
