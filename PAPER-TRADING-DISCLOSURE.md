# Paper-Trading Disclosure

ThesisCircuit is intended exclusively for Alpaca's paper-trading environment. Paper trading is a simulation: no actual securities transactions or real funds are involved. Results are hypothetical, do not represent actual trading, and do not guarantee future results.

Nothing in this project is investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security or cryptocurrency. Options trading carries significant risk and is not suitable for all investors; complex options strategies carry additional risk. All investments involve risk, including possible loss of principal.

Phase 0 places zero trades and contains no order-submission path.
