# ThesisCircuit — AI Logic, Risk Gates, and Alpaca Infrastructure

## AI logic

ThesisCircuit turns a market hypothesis into a structured options thesis through independent market, options, risk, and skeptic agents. Each agent emits an auditable stance, confidence, rationale, and evidence label. Consensus can advance a thesis to review but never grants execution authority.

## Risk gates

A deterministic governor rejects stale data, low confidence, excessive bounded loss, unsuitable expiration windows, live endpoints, non-paper modes, and any attempt to enable order submission. Vetoes are final and replayable. Phase 0 performs research evaluation only and places zero trades.

## Alpaca infrastructure

The final system is designed for Alpaca's paper-trading endpoint, a fresh dedicated judging account with a $100,000 starting balance, and Alpaca MCP or CLI inspection. Credentials remain server-side. The web interface presents the complete thesis, agent votes, risk verdict, and replay trail without exposing account secrets.

## Disclosure
